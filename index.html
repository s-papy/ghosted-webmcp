🪳 Roach Motel Exit

A subscription dashboard for a person and their AI agent. Cancel by hand and you get the maze every subscription business builds on purpose. Ask your agent to cancel through its WebMCP tools and it just happens — because this page tells the agent the truth about what "cancel" means, instead of hiding it behind a retention flow.

The average subscriber hits 6.2 dark patterns and 6.7 clicks to cancel a service — bad enough that the US FTC built a rule around it ("Click to Cancel"). Try the Cancel button below by hand, then ask an agent to cancel the same thing.

Everything lives in this browser's localStorage — no server, no real subscriptions, no real money, no connection to any real account.
