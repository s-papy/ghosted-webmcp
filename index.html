👻 Ghosted

A tracker that admits when someone's gone silent — a job application, or an unpaid invoice — and an agent that does the checking for you.

⚠ 53% of job seekers were ghosted by an employer in the past year, and it now takes ~40 applications to land one interview.

🤖 Try it with an agent

  1. Open this page in Chrome with chrome://flags/#enable-webmcp-testing turned on, or in ChatGPT's in-app browser.
  2. Then just ask, in plain language — no special syntax:
  • "Check my job pipeline for anything I should know about."
  • "Flag any applications that have gone silent."
  • "Log a new invoice for Acme Co — $800, due in 14 days."
  • "I just got an offer — who else should I notify?"

No agent handy right now? Everything below also works by hand — click Flag, use the dropdowns, click a card. But watching an agent do it in one call, live, is the whole point.

Technical details
Everything lives in this browser's localStorage — no server, no real applications, no real invoices, no email or bank access. Company and client names are fictional. Job tools: log_application, get_application, list_applications, search_applications, update_status, flag_stale_applications, get_followup_reminders, get_pipeline_summary, get_leverage_opportunities. Invoice tools: log_invoice, get_invoice, list_invoices, search_invoices, update_invoice_status, flag_stale_invoices, get_invoice_followup_reminders, get_payment_summary.
