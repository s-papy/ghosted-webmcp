Technical details
Everything lives in this browser's localStorage — no server, no real applications, no real invoices, no email or bank access. Company and client names are fictional. Job tools: log_application, get_application, list_applications, search_applications, update_status, flag_stale_applications, get_followup_reminders, get_pipeline_summary, get_leverage_opportunities. Invoice tools: log_invoice, get_invoice, list_invoices, search_invoices, update_invoice_status, flag_stale_invoices, get_invoice_followup_reminders, get_payment_summary.
